Roots English Farm

Role: Owner, designer and developer
Built with: Vue, Firebase, offline-friendly web app
View Live Site

The challenge

My family’s permaculture farm in Ban Pong needed a home online for visitors and future workshops. I also needed a way to keep track of what is planted in every bed, and of more than 300 tree saplings, without a spreadsheet.

What I built

A public website for the farm, and a private tracker at /farm/. Logging a planting with a photo takes seconds on a phone in the field, and visitors get a read-only view of what is growing.

The data model was designed first as a small local app, then moved online once it had proved itself in daily use.
