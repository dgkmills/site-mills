Workplace English course and portal

Role: Course writer, trainer and developer
Built with: Node.js, Vue, Firebase free plan
View Live Site

The challenge

The HR team at a Danish-owned factory in Thailand reports to head office in English: monthly updates, charts, pay decisions, meetings. General English classes weren’t moving that. The team needed practice on the exact situations they face, and management wanted to see progress.

What I built

A 20-class course, “Presenting in English”. Every class has the same five parts: a sound to fix, the language for the task, practice, a real work situation to speak through, and a one-line takeaway.

Each class is written once as a single source file. A build script turns it into the student handout, the teacher’s copy with answers and timings, and an online lesson. When I change a class, all three stay in step.

The online portal lets learners hear every word and model answer, record themselves and compare, and fill in exercises that check themselves. Managers see who has finished which lessons. Scores and recordings stay private to each learner.

How progress is shown

The course is running now. Each learner records a benchmark presentation in the first class and again near the end, so the change can be heard, not just described. The portal runs on Firebase’s free plan, so the client pays nothing for hosting.
