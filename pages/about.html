About me

I’m an American living in Ban Pong, Ratchaburi. I came to Thailand from Hawaii, where I worked in commercial horticulture and beekeeping, and I have a business degree from the University of Oregon.

Since 2019 I’ve taught English in Thailand: first through a teaching agency in schools across Ratchaburi and Nakhon Pathom, now with my own students, from young learners to office teams at a manufacturing company. I started building websites in 2025 because I wanted better tools for my own classes, and I still build them for real people I work with.

I build with AI coding tools and keep things simple on purpose: small, fast sites on free hosting that a business owner can understand and keep running. When I’m not teaching or building, I’m working on my family’s permaculture farm.
